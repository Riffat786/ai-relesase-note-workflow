# Investment Portfolio Tracking (AI)

Release 1.0 · July 2026

## Overview

**What it does:** Consolidate all your investment holdings (mutual funds, stocks, gold bonds, debt funds) into one portfolio view. See total value, total invested, total return %, XIRR, and individual investment details at a glance.

**Why it matters:** Previously, your investment holdings lived in separate apps and statements with no unified view. Now see your total invested value, performance, and asset allocation in one place.

**Key benefits:** Review individual holdings with complete details (name, type, units, NAV, value, return %). Export holdings to CSV for analysis. Receive AI-driven rebalancing alerts when your allocation deviates from targets, with specific recommendations.

## Workflow

1. Open WealthWise and navigate to the **Investments** module (available to Pro and Family tier users).
2. View the **portfolio summary** showing total value, total invested, total return %, and XIRR at a glance.
3. Scroll down to the **holdings table** to see each investment with its name, type, units, NAV, value, and return percentage.
4. Click **Export CSV** to download your holdings data for analysis in spreadsheets or other tools.
5. When an allocation deviates from your target, an **AI rebalancing alert** appears with specific recommendations based on your portfolio and goals.
6. Review the recommendation and decide whether to rebalance manually or adjust your targets accordingly.
