# Investment portfolio tracking (AI)

*Release 1.0 · July 2026*

---

### Overview

**What it does:** Consolidates your mutual funds, stocks, gold, and other holdings in one module with real-time portfolio analytics, performance tracking, and AI-generated insights.

**Why it matters:** Previously, tracking investments across multiple providers meant juggling separate apps or spreadsheets. Now you see your complete financial picture in WealthWise with actionable AI-driven analysis.

**Key benefits:** View all holdings in one consolidated dashboard. Track portfolio performance against benchmarks over time. Receive AI-generated insights on diversification, risk assessment, and rebalancing opportunities. Monitor category and asset allocation at a glance.

---

### Workflow

1. Open the Investments module from the main navigation. WealthWise displays your portfolio overview, showing total value and asset allocation across categories.

2. Add your holdings by selecting **Add investment**. Enter the type (mutual fund, stock, or gold), name, quantity, and purchase price. WealthWise records the transaction and updates your consolidated portfolio.

3. Review your full portfolio on the Investments dashboard. You see all holdings grouped by type, current market value, gains or losses, and your total invested amount.

4. View portfolio analytics by selecting **Analytics**. Charts show your asset allocation, performance over time, category breakdown, and comparison to your target allocation.

5. Review AI-generated insights in the **Insights** panel. WealthWise analyzes your holdings and presents recommendations—for example, rebalancing suggestions if one category is overweight, or diversification opportunities based on your risk profile.

6. Update your holdings as needed. Edit quantities, add new investments, or record sales. WealthWise recalculates analytics and insights in real time.
