# AI Financial Advisor chat interface (AI)

*Release 1.0 · July 2026*

---

### Overview

**What it does:** Conversational AI assistant accessible from your dashboard sidebar that answers questions about finances, suggests budget adjustments, recommends investments, and alerts you to spending anomalies.

**Why it matters:** Get personalized financial guidance instantly without scheduling appointments or consulting external advisors. The AI learns your spending patterns and provides timely, relevant advice.

**Key benefits:**
- Get instant answers to financial questions anytime
- Receive budget optimization suggestions based on your spending habits
- Discover investment recommendations tailored to your goals
- Get proactive alerts when unusual spending patterns are detected

---

### Workflow

1. Open your dashboard and locate the AI Advisor in the sidebar, then click to open the chat interface.
2. Type your financial question into the chat box (for example, "How can I save more on groceries?" or "Should I invest in index funds?").
3. Read the AI Advisor's personalized response.
4. Review the suggested budget adjustments, investment recommendations, or spending insights.
5. Take action on any recommendations or ask follow-up questions within the chat.
6. Monitor proactive alerts from the AI Advisor when it detects spending anomalies or potential financial risks.
