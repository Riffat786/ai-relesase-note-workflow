# Investment Portfolio Tracking

Release 1.0 · July 2026

## Overview

- **See your total portfolio value, total invested, total return (%), and XIRR in a single dashboard** — consolidate all your investment holdings (mutual funds, stocks, gold bonds, debt funds) in one place without switching between multiple platforms.
- **Review individual holdings with complete details** — each investment shows name, type, units, NAV, value, and return percentage so you understand exactly how each position is performing.
- **Export holdings to CSV and get AI-driven rebalancing alerts** — download your data for further analysis, and receive notifications when your allocation deviates from your target with specific recommendations.

## Workflow

1. Open WealthWise and navigate to the **Investments** module (available to Pro and Family tier users).
2. View the **portfolio summary** showing total value, total invested, total return %, and XIRR at a glance.
3. Scroll down to the **holdings table** to see each investment with its name, type, units, NAV, value, and return percentage.
4. Click **Export CSV** to download your holdings data for analysis in spreadsheets or other tools.
5. When an allocation deviates from your target, an **AI rebalancing alert** appears with specific recommendations based on your portfolio and goals.
6. Review the recommendation and decide whether to rebalance manually or adjust your targets accordingly.
