# AI Financial Advisor Chat Interface (AI)

Release 1.0 · July 2026

## Overview

- **Get specific, numeric guidance on spending, savings, goals, and investments** without navigating multiple screens — ask your AI Advisor conversational questions and receive actionable recommendations instantly.
- **Ask follow-up questions and have the context of your financial data available immediately** — the advisor uses your transaction, budget, goal, and investment data to answer naturally.
- **Preserve your conversation history** as you explore different financial topics and receive personalized recommendations based on your full financial picture.

## Workflow

1. Open the WealthWise app and click **Ask AI Advisor** from the sidebar, or access it from the Overview, Budget, or Investments view.
2. The chat window opens, showing your message history and starter question chips (such as "Should I increase my emergency fund?").
3. Type a natural-language question or select a starter question to send.
4. Watch the typing indicator while the AI processes your transaction, budget, goal, and investment data.
5. The AI responds with specific, numeric guidance highlighted in purple, tailored to your financial situation.
6. Ask follow-up questions to dive deeper, or start a new topic — your entire conversation history is preserved for future reference.
