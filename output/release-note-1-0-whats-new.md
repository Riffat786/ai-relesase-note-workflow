## 🚀 What's New

### AI Financial Advisor Chat Interface (AI)

Previously, you relied on manual dashboard checks and static guidance to understand your finances. Now, your AI Financial Advisor is available right from your sidebar, ready to answer questions, suggest budget adjustments, recommend investments, and proactively alert you to spending anomalies. It's like having a financial advisor available 24/7.

**Key Benefits:**
- Get personalized financial guidance instantly without leaving the app
- Receive proactive alerts when your spending shows unusual patterns
- Access investment recommendations tailored to your financial profile
- Get actionable budget adjustment suggestions based on your patterns

[Learn more →](help-topic-1-ai-financial-advisor-chat-interface.html)

### Investment Portfolio Tracking (AI)

Previously, your investment holdings were scattered across multiple apps and statements. Now, consolidate all your holdings—mutual funds, stocks, gold, and more—in one dedicated Investments module. Track performance, view portfolio analytics, and benefit from AI-generated insights to optimize your investments.

**Key Benefits:**
- See all your investments in one place with complete performance tracking
- Understand your portfolio composition with detailed analytics
- Receive AI-powered insights to improve your investment strategy
- Monitor your wealth growth across all asset classes

[Learn more →](help-topic-2-investment-portfolio-tracking.html)

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

| Bug ID | Description | Fix / Solution |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| #5 | Category and total budget values above ₹1,00,000 displayed with incorrect rounding, causing discrepancies between dashboard totals and transaction sums. | Budget totals now match the exact sum of transactions to the rupee across all value ranges. |
| #6 | Some users received two identical bill reminder notifications for the same recurring bill. | Duplicate notifications have been eliminated; you now receive exactly one reminder per scheduled bill. |
| #7 | When navigating back from Onboarding Step 4 to Step 3, slider values reset to defaults instead of retaining user input. | Slider state now persists across forward and back navigation, retaining your inputs when you return to previous steps. |

## Known Issues

- **Delayed sync for linked brokerage holdings:** Holdings synced from certain third-party brokerage connections (e.g., Zerodha) may experience a two to four hour sync delay before appearing in your portfolio. New holdings may not appear immediately after linking a brokerage account.
