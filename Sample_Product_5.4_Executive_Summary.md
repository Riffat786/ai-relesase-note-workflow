## Key Business Value

### Security Enhancement (High Priority)
**Multi-Factor Authentication** directly addresses enterprise security requirements and compliance mandates. This feature enables organizations to meet modern security standards and reduce credential-based attack surface.

- **Use Case:** Protecting administrative and sensitive data access
- **Customer Segment:** All enterprise customers, mandatory for regulated industries
- **ROI:** Reduced security incidents, improved compliance posture

### Operational Efficiency (Medium Priority)
**Dashboard Export to Excel** improves productivity by enabling faster reporting and analysis workflows without manual data transfer.

- **Use Case:** Business reporting, executive dashboards, BI integration
- **Customer Segment:** Operations, Finance, Business Intelligence teams
- **ROI:** Reduced reporting preparation time, faster decision-making

### Reliability Improvement (Ongoing)
**Large Report Export Fixes** eliminate blocker issues preventing critical workflows.

- **Use Case:** Comprehensive data export and archival
- **Customer Segment:** All users generating large reports
- **ROI:** Improved system reliability, reduced support tickets
